Essential Volunteer Guidelines
  • Only registered NGOs make formal requests; volunteers assist with pickup, inspection and transit.
  • Always verify food condition, packing, and temperature before signing off on pickup.
  • Maintain direct communication with donor and NGO coordinators via the contact details provided.
  • Confirm delivery promptly upon arrival to update the live chain of custody and earn Green Credits.
